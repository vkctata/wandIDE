# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Wand is built for thoughtful software teams. We want participation in the
project to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Welcome different backgrounds, perspectives, and experience levels.
- Keep discussions relevant, safe, and free from harassment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, sexualized
content directed at others, and disruptive or abusive conduct are not welcome.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately to the maintainers through the security contact in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as
confidentially as practical.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
